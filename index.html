About

Marketing Scholar & Educator

Dr. Britton R. Leggett is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at McNeese State University, where he teaches undergraduate and graduate courses spanning digital marketing, consumer behavior, and marketing analytics.

His scholarship bridges the gap between traditional marketing theory and the realities of today's data-driven, socially connected marketplace. Dr. Leggett's work has been published in peer-reviewed journals and presented at national conferences, with a focus on how emerging technologies — including artificial intelligence and social media — are reshaping consumer decision-making and firm strategy.

Prior to his academic career, Dr. Leggett gained professional experience in the business world, which informs his applied, practitioner-oriented teaching philosophy. He is committed to preparing students to think critically and act strategically in evolving markets.

Research Interests

Investigating the intersection of technology, consumer behavior, and modern marketing strategy.

Marketing Analytics

Quantitative approaches to understanding consumer behavior, market dynamics, and the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, including predictive modeling and data visualization.

Social Media & Influencers

How social media platforms and digital influencers shape consumer attitudes, brand perceptions, and purchasing decisions across B2C and B2B contexts.

Sports Marketing

Fan engagement, brand equity in professional sports, ticket demand forecasting, and how leagues leverage digital channels to build loyalty beyond in-person attendance.

AI in Marketing

The strategic and pedagogical implications of artificial intelligence, examining how firms deploy intelligent tools and how marketing education must evolve for future professionals.

Marketing Education

Curriculum innovation for the digital age, including how educators can integrate emerging platforms, analytics tools, and real-world case work into the modern classroom.

Brand Equity & Identity

How brands build and maintain value through consistent messaging, community building, and alignment with consumer values, especially in fragmented media environments.
